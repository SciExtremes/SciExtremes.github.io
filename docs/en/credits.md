## Background image

File: `assets/images/hero/camp-fire.jpg`.

Source: [NASA Earth Observatory — Camp Fire Rages in California](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/camp-fire-rages-in-california-144225/). Landsat 8 OLI visible and shortwave-infrared imagery highlights active fire areas.

Attribution: NASA Earth Observatory image by Joshua Stevens, using Landsat data from the U.S. Geological Survey.

The visible source label and URL are configured in `data/home.yaml`. When replacing the background, update the source and attribution. See NASA's [Images and Media Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
