## New publication

Our Communications Earth & Environment paper examines fire weather waves: persistent periods of extreme fire weather that sustain conditions conducive to burning.

## A disproportionate share of fire activity

In forested ecoregions, these episodes account for only 4% of days but coincide with 26% of burned area and nearly half of the top 1% most energetic fires. Forest fires show a stronger and more persistent response than grassland and shrubland fires.

## Why it matters

The findings connect the duration of extreme weather with wildfire activity. They support considering persistence in early warning and preparedness, alongside the severity of individual days. The paper and open published-version PDF are linked below.
