## Research in the news

Our Science Advances study examines increasing synchronicity of extreme fire weather within and across regions. It considers climate drivers and links with fire-sourced air pollution.

## Why the findings matter

Firefighting assistance often depends on regions having different periods of high demand. More simultaneous extreme fire weather can put pressure on that coordination, while smoke connects fire conditions with health impacts beyond the fire perimeter.
