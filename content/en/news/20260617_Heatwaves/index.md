## New publication

Research in Science Advances examines wildfire occurrence and growth across the western United States during 2001–2024. Forty-two percent of burned area occurred during and immediately following heatwaves.

## Beyond the hottest days

The analysis shows that effects can persist after a heatwave ends, linking meteorological conditions and fuel flammability with ongoing fire activity. In forests, about 64% of the increase in burned area since 2001 coincided with heatwaves; nonforests did not show the same pattern.
