## Persistence

The seminar introduced fire weather waves and examined how sustained extreme fire weather relates to fire activity across terrestrial ecoregions.

## Synchronicity

It also considered concurrent extreme fire weather within and across regions, its climate drivers, and implications for air quality and firefighting coordination.
