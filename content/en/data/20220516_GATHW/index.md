## Archived data

The public archive contains global daily apparent temperature and heatwave records for 2006–2020. It is derived from ERA5 temperature, humidity, wind and solar-radiation variables.

## Toolbox

GATHW supports three apparent-temperature methods and spatial resolutions of 0.25°, 0.5° and 1°. Its interface allows users to choose dates, methods, units and spatial extent before inspecting or exporting results.

## Release scope

The paper describes a near-real-time workflow, while the linked archive is the published 2006–2020 release. The archive should not be interpreted as a continuously updated observational service.

## Access and citation

Download the archived files from Zenodo and consult the Geoscience Data Journal paper for methods and evaluation. Cite the data release and the associated publication.
