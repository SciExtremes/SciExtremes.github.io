## What it provides

CETD detects and visualises compound climate events and measures their frequency, duration and severity. It supports multivariate, sequential and spatially concurrent event definitions rather than treating every extreme as an isolated hazard.

## Inputs and applications

The framework works with observations, reanalysis and climate-model simulations. Examples include hot-dry, wet-windy and hot-dry-stagnation events. The paper demonstrates applications to the 2019 Australian wildfires and the 2022 European heatwaves.

## Workflow

Prepare the input variables, select event thresholds and duration criteria, detect events, and inspect the resulting statistics and maps. Definitions should be selected for the research question and reported alongside the results.

## Access and citation

The Figshare archive contains the toolbox and accompanying datasets. Consult the archive for release information and the Scientific Data paper for methods and evaluation. Cite the release used as well as the associated paper.
