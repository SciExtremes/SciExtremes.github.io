## Data and methods

GHWT combines historical climate data from CRU-JRA, ERA5 and GLDAS with GFDL-ESM4 projections under three SSP scenarios. The study constructs global heatwave records for 1971–2100.

## Event definitions

Absolute and percentile temperature thresholds are combined with duration criteria. The Python source supports user-defined thresholds, allowing event definitions to be matched to a particular application.

## Interpreting the coverage

The record combines historical analysis and future projections. Future years represent scenario-dependent simulations rather than observations; the related paper documents the sources, definitions and evaluation.
