> Centered on the underlying mechanisms driving extreme climate events, compound risks, and their pervasive impacts on socio-ecological systems, this research portfolio encompasses three core domains:

### 🌪️ Compound Extreme Events and Physical Mechanisms

Investigating the spatiotemporal superposition and underlying physical linkages among diverse extreme climate events. This research spearheaded the development of the global Compound Events Detection and Visualisation Toolbox (CETD), offering a standardized framework for the systematic, quantitative assessment of compound risks arising from concurrent meteorological hazards.

**📚 Related Publications:**
* [CETD, a global compound events detection and visualisation toolbox and dataset](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04530-x)

---

### 🌍 Socioeconomic Exposure and Adaptation Strategies

Assessing the spatiotemporal dynamics of heatwaves globally and across critical regions (e.g., the Belt and Road initiative) under various projected warming scenarios, thereby quantifying the associated socioeconomic exposure risks. Concurrently, leveraging "Climate Analog" methodologies, this work establishes an empirical, scientific baseline to inform future climate adaptation and urban planning initiatives across Chinese municipalities.

**📚 Related Publications:**
* [Spatiotemporal Distribution and Risk Assessment of Heat Waves Based on Apparent Temperature in the One Belt and One Road Region](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs12071174)
* [Analogs of Future Climate in Chinese Cities Identified in Present Observations](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3042548)

---

### 🌱 Land Surface Processes and Ecosystem Responses

Diagnosing the principal climatological drivers governing spring vegetation phenology at regional scales (e.g., the Qinling Mountains), and investigating how snow cover variations in the Northern Hemisphere perturb the radiative balance of the land-atmosphere system. Ultimately, this research elucidates the cascading effects of climate anomalies on both physical land surface processes and broader ecosystem dynamics.

**📚 Related Publications:**
* [Diagnose the dominant climate factors and periods of spring phenology in Qinling Mountains, China](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolind.2021.108211)
* [Contribution of Changes in Snow Cover Extent to Shortwave Radiation Perturbations at the Top of the Atmosphere over the Northern Hemisphere during 2000–2019](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs13234938)
